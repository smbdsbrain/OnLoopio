# Controls

The Y1 wheel browses lists. Press the centre button to open a selection; a double press starts playback, and a hold opens actions for a track, album or playlist. On the Now Playing screen, a centre press switches between volume and seeking; rotate the wheel to adjust after one full turn arms it. The lower hardware button plays or pauses after a 420 ms double-press window. Two quick presses toggle the current track's like without changing playback. Four quick centre presses toggle the control lock.

**Like / Remove like** is available in a track's actions and the player menu. **Library → Favorite tracks** shows liked music, with hearts marking liked tracks. Offline favorites include only playable downloaded and local files. Likes for files in `Music` stay on the device. Liking a track does not download it or protect it from cache cleanup.

The player menu leads back to Library, Download queue, Settings and Power off. Settings also exposes key lock, screen timeout, brightness, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and cache options. If the controls appear unresponsive, check the lock state and wake the screen.

Closing an action menu returns to the item that opened it, keeping its position on screen. Back and the menu's Back row behave the same way. Library lists remember their own selections when returning from a child list or Settings. If an item moves during synchronization, selection follows it; if it disappears, selection moves to the nearest remaining row.
